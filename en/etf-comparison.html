Method and limits

Monthly return = (1 + effective annual return)^(1/12) − 1. Apply the return, then add the contribution every month; no intermediate rounding.

Assume total returns with distributions reinvested. Separate cash distributions, FX, taxes, trading costs and volatility paths are not modelled. Use one currency for all inputs.

TQQQ targets 3x the DAILY Nasdaq-100 performance, not 3x its long-term return. This constant-rate model does not reproduce daily leverage resets or volatility effects.

What to check in issuer documents

Compare the same as-of date, total return versus price return, NAV versus market price, fees, distributions and holdings. Historical returns are not forecasts; also test loss assumptions.
